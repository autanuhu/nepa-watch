# ⚡ NEPA Watch

### Open Electricity Outage Intelligence for Africa

NEPA Watch is an open-source platform designed to collect, analyze, and visualize electricity outage information from communities.

The goal is simple:

**Turn everyday electricity experiences into useful open data and intelligence.**


NEPA Watch will begin with electricity outage reporting in Nigeria and grow toward a broader African electricity intelligence platform.

---

## 🎯 The Problem

Electricity reliability affects homes, businesses, schools, hospitals, farms, and industries every day.

However, electricity outage information is often:

- Fragmented
- Difficult to verify
- Not easily accessible
- Not available in a useful historical format
- Difficult for developers and researchers to analyze

NEPA Watch aims to create an open infrastructure for collecting and understanding this information.

---

## 💡 What NEPA Watch Will Provide

Users will eventually be able to report:

- ⚡ Power outages
- 🔌 Power restoration
- 📍 Location
- 🕐 Outage duration
- 📊 Electricity reliability
- 🚨 Infrastructure problems
- 📝 Community observations

The platform will transform these reports into useful intelligence.
